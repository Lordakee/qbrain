  COALESCE(CAST(lock_until AS TEXT),'') (identity on SQLite; required on
  PG where lock_until is timestamptz and a bare '' cannot cast). The
  census had classified these statements portable under the TEXT-column
  assumption; A's timestamptz DDL falsified that. Recorded here as a
  census erratum.
- schema/001_init.sql was STALE vs the committed schema_sql.hpp (jobs
  table missing lock_token/error_text columns). Synced before
  regeneration so the canonical v1 DDL stays byte-identical to the
  committed header apart from the sanctioned n38 changes.
- scripts/gen-schema-header.ps1 under Windows PowerShell 5.1 emits a BOM
  and mangles the em dash; regenerated with pwsh (PowerShell 7) instead.
- scripts/build-cl.ps1 / build-tests-cl.ps1 link libpq.lib without
  /LIBPATH:"<PgRoot>\lib" (LNK1181). Worked around in the private tree
  via LIB=<PgRoot>\lib; repo fix belongs to A's build wiring.
